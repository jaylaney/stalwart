### Findings

#### F10 — important, high: the revision 4 port of `test_attachment_indexer` unindexes live messages, which erases their metadata rows, so the requeued passes index nothing and the control proves nothing

Plan lines 595–602 (Task 8 step 3, the ported control; the instruction at 598) and 792
(the R8-again disposition).

Evidence:
- At 1.0 an unindex queue entry is the second half of a destroy. The worker calls
  `delete_email_metadata(server, batch, account_id, document_id, row)`
  (`v1.0.3:crates/services/src/index_queue/index.rs:378-379`, `:835-857`), which runs
  `metadata.unindex(batch, &thread_subject)`: that clears `EmailField::Metadata`,
  `EmailField::SortKeys`, the hash index property and the blob's `BlobLink::Document`
  (`v1.0.3:crates/email/src/message/index/metadata.rs:39-56`). It does not check that the
  message was destroyed.
- The index pass then reads the metadata rows of its items and, for an insert whose row is
  missing, calls `nothing_to_index` and continues (`index.rs:343-350`), before
  `build_email_document` is ever reached.
- The case's `delete_blobs_and_requeue` reads each message's row with
  `stored_metadata(..).unarchive().unwrap()` to find the blob hash
  (`fork:tests/src/za/index_task.rs:566-575`).

Failing scenario: the ported control queues `queue_document_unindex` for both accounts'
messages, commits, and waits for the queues. Both messages are still in their mailboxes, so the
unindex deletes their metadata rows, sort keys and blob links. The next step,
`delete_blobs_and_requeue`, panics on the first missing row; if that read were made tolerant,
the requeued passes would find no row and skip both documents without reading anything: the
key account's "ids-only document recreated" assertion fails on a correct server, and the
ordinary control's "no document, no `QueueStatus`" holds vacuously, without exercising
upstream's missing-blob skip at all. The expectation-ledger line would then record a behaviour
the case never observed.

Correction: remove the search documents at store level, not through the queue. The helper
exists: `unindex_search_account(test, SearchIndex::Email, account_id)`
(`fork:tests/src/utils/za.rs:2672-2677`, `Store::unindex_account`, the range delete account
destruction runs over the search store only) for both accounts, after the positive control's
assertion and before `delete_blobs_and_requeue`. It touches no message row or blob link, so the
requeue finds the rows, the ordinary pass reaches `build_email_document`, reads the deleted
blob and takes the `Ok(None)` skip (`v1.0.3:crates/services/src/index_queue/document.rs:35-48`),
and the key account's pass recreates its ids-only document from the row. Do not use
`emulate_superseding_node` (`index_task.rs:983`) either: it also calls `metadata.unindex`. Keep
the rest of the ported assertions as revision 4 states them.

#### F11 — important, medium: the codec teardown retires the key while blobs the recoder never selects may still be encrypted under it, and every later leak scan decodes every blob in the store

Plan lines 571–580 (teardown order) and 603–607 (`leak::test_codec`); also 588–594.

Evidence:
- `user_blob_hashes` selects a committed blob only through a document link of `Email`,
  `SieveScript` or `FileNode`, an id link, or a temporary link that is an archived item
  (`v1.0.3:crates/store/src/write/blob.rs:328-374`); in the OSS build `is_archived_item` is
  `false` (`:383-386`), so every plain temporary hold is skipped. Temporary-only holds written
  during the window: spam training samples (`v1.0.3:crates/email/src/message/ingest.rs:1177`,
  `crates/spam-filter/src/modules/classifier.rs:1023`,
  `crates/jmap/src/registry/mapping/spam_sample.rs:168,222`), a staged blob of an ingest that
  did not commit, an upload not yet imported. A blob unlinked before the purge is skipped too.
- The scanner collects every key of `Subspace::Blobs` and every `BlobLink` hash of the whole
  store, not the scanned account's (`fork:tests/src/za/leak.rs:1327-1342`), and decodes each
  through the server's read (`:1515-1532`, Task 7's port); a decode error is a violation,
  "blob does not decode". `leak::test`, `leak::test_index_control` and the mail modules' scans
  run after `test_codec`.
- The purge that would remove unlinked and expired blobs is not in the teardown;
  `expire_and_purge_blobs` exists (`fork:tests/src/utils/za.rs:1027`).

Failing scenario: `leak::test_codec` delivers its canaries with zstd and the key set; a
delivery holds a training sample or a staged blob under the key with a temporary link only.
The teardown recodes back (which skips that blob), destroys the messages, destroys the key and
reloads. The hold expires or stays; the blob stays encrypted under a key no keyring holds.
`leak::test`'s scan, a few modules later, reports "blob does not decode" for a hash no test
named, and the failure is attributed to the wrong module; if the blob survives to the suite's
end, `assert_is_empty`'s `Blobs` leftover rule reports it too.

Correction, in Task 8 step 3's teardown order:
- between steps 3 and 4, run `expire_and_purge_blobs` (expire every temporary link, purge),
  then, with the key still loaded, a whole-store decode check: every hash of `Subspace::Blobs`
  and `BlobLink` decodes through `test.server.get_blob` with no error;
- after step 4 (key destroyed, settings restored, `reload_settings`, `reload_core()`), repeat
  the decode check with the retired keyring. Zero "does not decode" is the module's exit
  condition and the detector for this class.
- Note in the plan why: the recoder cannot reach a temporary-only or unlinked blob, so the
  proof that the key is safe to retire is the post-retirement scan, not the recode's event.

#### F14 — minor, high: "no captured event holds the sender address" cannot hold while SMTP events carry the envelope sender

Plan lines 383, 389 and 393.

Evidence: the inbound SMTP events trace `From = <the MAIL FROM address>`
(`fork:crates/smtp/src/inbound/mail.rs:141,201,233,471,494`), and the queue and delivery
events name recipients and senders; invariant 47's wording accepts addresses in queue events.
The cases send from `dmarc-ac-*@remote.test` as both envelope and header sender.

Scenario: the capture starts before the message is submitted; the assertion finds the sender
in `Smtp(MailFrom)` and fails on a correct server, and the implementer narrows it ad hoc.

Correction: state the scope: no `Encryption(*)` event is captured for the case, and no event
of any type carries the key's fingerprint; or give the message a header `From` distinct from
its envelope sender and assert the header address appears in no event. The same scoping
applies to `gateway_events_carry_no_content` (line 407).

#### F15 — minor, high: decision 2 does not say what the mixed-recipient rule discloses

Plan lines 34–46 (decision 2), 385 (`harvest_mixed_recipients`) and 696–700 (Task 11's
invariant wording).

Evidence: when the harvest runs, `harvest_message` writes the sender's key, source and
`last_seen` into the plaintext `ExternalPublicKey` row and traces `Encryption(KeyHarvested)`
or `KeyChange` with `From = <header From address>` and the fingerprint
(`v1.0.3:crates/common/src/gateway/harvest.rs:110-149`). Under the per-recipient rule a
message delivered to a key account and a plain co-recipient is harvested, so the key account's
correspondent's header `From` (not only the envelope) is traced and its key and sighting are
stored in the clear, keyed by that address.

Correction: add the consequence to decision 2's text so Jay decides with it in view, and have
Task 11's invariant wording name it beside the queue events' addresses: the header `From` of a
message a plain co-recipient harvested, and its key and last-seen time, are operator-readable.

#### F16 — minor, medium: `harvest_skips_pending_account` leaves a retrying queue message behind

Plan lines 391–394.

Evidence: a refused delivery is a temporary failure the queue retries (the fence cases note a
retry falls due a second after the attempt, `fork:tests/src/za/mail/fence.rs:371-377`); every
retry re-runs the gate (each answers `false`, which is fine) and, once the module destroys the
account, the next attempt is a permanent failure whose DSN goes to `remote.test`, the sink.

Scenario: the message is still queued when the module returns; the next module's
`wait_for_delivery` waits on it, or its `sink()` count includes the DSN.

Correction: before destroying the account, delete the queued message (or let the delivery
succeed after `setup` and clear it), then `wait_for_queue`; say so in the case.

### F1 — important, high: the blob-codec cases remove a `SymmetricKey` while blobs still depend on it, and the harness's `Server` snapshot never sees the key

Plan lines 504, 513–531 (Task 8 `test_blob_recode_keeps_sealed`, `leak::test_codec`) and
481–483 (Task 7, the scanner reads through `test.server.get_blob`).

Evidence:
- `fork:tests/src/utils/server.rs:497-499`: `TestServer::reload_core` rebuilds `self.server`
  from `inner`; until it is called, `test.server` is the `Server` built at suite start, whose
  `core.blob.keys` is the empty keyring. `fork:tests/src/utils/registry.rs:264-267`
  (`reload_settings`) reloads the running server's core, not that snapshot.
- `fork:tests/src/utils/storage.rs:215-262`: `wait_for_tasks` (which `wait_for_delivery`,
  `destroy_key_accounts` and most modules call) loops until no search-index partition "still
  holds queue entries, a queued-work marker or a failure status", with no deadline.
- `v1.0.3:crates/services/src/index_queue/document.rs` (merge `:46`): an ordinary account's
  index build reads the blob with `server.get_blob`; a decrypt error is an `Err`, not the
  missing-blob `Ok(None)` skip, so the partition fails and retries with backoff
  (CLAUDE.md: "a failing partition retries with backoff ... and never fails terminally").
- `v1.0.3:crates/services/src/task_manager/tasks/blob_recode.rs:61-62`:
  `TaskBlobRecodeCompression::Disable` means `CompressionAlgo::None` for every blob
  `user_blob_hashes` selects (every Email, SieveScript, FileNode and queue blob of every
  account on the server), and `:69-74` `Encrypt(target)` names a key id the plan omits.

Failing scenario 1 (`leak::test_codec`): the module sets `Email.blobEncryptionKey`, reloads,
delivers its canaries, and scans with `test.server.get_blob(...)`. `test.server` still holds
the empty keyring, so every encrypted blob answers "blob does not decode" (the scanner's
violation branch, `fork:tests/src/za/leak.rs:1527-1530`): the case fails on a correct
server, and M8's expected failure is indistinguishable from it.

Failing scenario 2 (either case): the key is removed (the `SymmetricKey` destroyed and the
setting restored) while `codec-leak-plain`'s messages, or any blob the module wrote while the
key was set, are still encrypted under it or still queued for indexing. The next index pass
of the Email partition fails with a decrypt error and retries forever; the next
`wait_for_delivery` or `wait_for_tasks` anywhere in the suite (the mail modules that follow,
`destroy_key_accounts` at the end) never returns, and the za suite times out with no
failing test named.

Failing scenario 3 (`test_blob_recode_keeps_sealed`): `BlobRecode { Disable, Disable }`
"restores trailer `0x00`" for the one blob under test by rewriting every linked blob on the
server uncompressed, and leaves the server's blobs in a state no other test expects (the
leak scans' raw-value search over `Blobs`, `fork:tests/src/za/leak.rs:1332`, was blind to LZ4
and now reads plaintext in ordinary accounts' blobs).

Correction:
- In both cases call `test.reload_core()` after each `reload_settings` (after the key is
  set and after it is removed), and state it in the plan; the same holds for any test-side
  read of `test.server.core.email.gateway` in Task 5's module.
- Fix the order: wait for the accounts' index queues (`wait_for_index_queue`) and for the
  recode's shard tasks, then clear or destroy the module's messages (and recode back with
  `encryption: Disable, compression: Keep`), and only then destroy the `SymmetricKey` and
  restore the settings. Assert the encrypted bit is cleared rather than trailer `== 0x00`.
- Name the key id in `Encrypt(target)`, and record in the outcome note that the recode
  touched every account's blobs.

### F3 — important, medium: `server_wide_tasks_do_not_block_setup` cannot hold its fixture, and a not-due task hangs every wait helper
