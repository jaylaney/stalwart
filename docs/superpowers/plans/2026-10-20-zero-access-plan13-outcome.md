# Zero-access mail: plan 13 outcome

Written 2026-10-21 at the end of plan 13's implementation (`2026-10-20-zero-access-13-mail.md`, Tasks 1-10).
Plan 13 builds release 3 (spec `2026-10-20-zero-access-mail-design.md`, revision 4): every message stored for a key account is a sealed blob plus a stub metadata archive, whatever wrote it (delivery, Sieve, APPEND, Email/set, Email/import, copy); IMAP and POP3 sessions hold the session keys for the life of the authenticated connection and open messages on read; searches, sorts, snippets, copies across accounts, sharing and raw reads that would need sealed content are refused with a clear error; and the traces of a key account's mail carry no content. The whole-branch review ran on fable after Task 10; its findings, the fix waves and rulings R46-R57 are in "Final review" at the end of this note.

## What changed and why

**Task 1, the primitives (spec 3.1, 3.2, 5.1-5.3).** `vault` gains `derive_mail_key` and `SessionKeys::mwk()` (the MK expanded under `za/v1/mail`, independent of the EWK) and, under `test_mode`, the live-keys counter. `groupware`'s sealing helpers become `pub` with raw-byte variants (`seal_padded`, `open_padded`; `seal_bytes_as` is now `seal_padded` plus base64 and produces the same bytes). `email::message::za::seal` holds the mail sealing module: `new_dek`, `seal_mail_envelope` and `open_mail_envelope` (`MailWrap::{Session, Public}`), `seal_mail_blob` and `open_mail_blob`, `seal_stub`, `is_stub` (the format byte at the head of `raw_headers`: the one discriminator), `open_stub`, and the `*_needs_private_key` probes. The associated data binds the envelope and the blob to the account and the bundle to the blob hash.

**Task 2, sessions (spec 3.3, 4).** IMAP `Session::authenticate` and POP3 `handle_auth` call `authenticate_with_keys`, attach the keys to the session's token and refuse a keyless login to a key account (OAUTHBEARER, XOAUTH2, API keys, the master user) with `NO [NOPERM]` / `-ERR` and the text `Zero-access accounts log in to mail with the password or an app password.` plus a `Security(Unauthorized)` trace; `refresh_access_token` re-attaches the session's own keys. The refusal block lives in a `common::za` helper so each upstream site is a few lines. The constants of the spec's fixed strings live in `common::za`.

**Task 3, sealed ingest (spec 5, 6.1, 6.2).** `index_message` is split into the metadata builder (`MessageMetadata::build` since the final review's F7, which put upstream's body back in place) and `index_metadata`; `email_ingest` gains one branch for key accounts (helpers in `za/ingest.rs`: `za_ingest_wrap`, `za_stage_sealed`, `za_index_stub`): a delivery without keys seals under the served public key (`pk`; no served key is a temporary failure), a session write seals under the mail key (`mk`), a keyless session write is refused (ruling R22), `IngestSource::Restore` is refused with a 550. The sealed blob is staged fresh (never the queue's or an upload's hash), the stub is written through upstream's metadata batch, and no thread, duplicate, merge, sample, PGP or iMIP step runs.

**Task 4, the provisioning fence (spec 6.1, 6.6).** Every ordinary ingest and copy batch ends with `assert_value(ZeroAccessVault, None)` (ruling R2); `setup-token` refuses PostgreSQL and MySQL, extends its emptiness check to mail (since ruling R46 also a destroyed message whose metadata archive awaits its unindex task), rechecks after writing the pending record and deletes that record conditionally on its own bytes (`Server::za_vault_delete`) when mail arrived in between; a reissue repairs a record left without a marker (ruling R28). `copy_message` refuses any cross-account copy into or out of a key account. The review added the same fence to `itip_ingest`'s batches and `commit_itip_merge` (ruling R25). Tests race ingest, copy and iMIP against provisioning with three pause hooks, and a stress test races ingest batches against record writes.

**Task 5, the readers (spec 5.5, 7).** One `za_metadata_view` (a `Cow<Archive<AlignedBytes>>`, built as `unseal_event_archive` builds its view, with an unversioned archive that fails closed as an assertion value) and one blob opener (`za_open_message_blob`, `Zeroizing`) at IMAP FETCH, POP3 RETR and TOP, Email/get, SearchSnippet/get, EmailSubmission/set, `blob_download`, with the keys taken lazily so content-free reads work without them (ruling R33); the index task builds the visible-field document (arrival time and size only, no blob read); push converts to state-only. A stub is read as one only when the reader holds keys (ruling R29). The unit test `the_view_keeps_upstreams_offsets` runs a six-message corpus; `reads::offsets_round_trip` compares a key and a plain account's FETCH, Email/get and download answers.

**Task 6, uploads and raw consumers (spec 5.7, 9).** A key account's uploads are sealed under `mk` (`za_put_sealed_upload`); `/jmap/upload` refuses a token without keys with a 403, and by ruling R30 so does `/jmap/download`; `Blob/upload` answers `forbidden`; `blob_download` opens a sealed upload with the keys. `FileNode/set` and `/copy`, `Blob/upload` data sources, `Blob/copy` and `x:SpamTrainingSample/set` refuse a sealed source before reading it (`za_refuse_raw_source`; spec 5.7 and 9 name the file and data-source consumers, ruling R32 adds spam training); `SieveScript/set` refuses only a message or section, not the account's own upload (R32 amendment).

**Task 7, the gates (spec 4, 6.3, 9).** A dispatcher gate answers `forbidden` for key-needing JMAP methods (`Email/get`, `/parse`, `SearchSnippet/get`, `Blob/get`, `Blob/upload`, `Email/import`, `Email/copy`, `EmailSubmission/set`, `Email/set` create) on a token without keys; cross-account IMAP COPY/MOVE and JMAP `Email/copy`/`Blob/copy` and grants and widenings of a key account's mailbox ACL are refused; a listing filter in `build_access_token` and a grantee invalidation at provisioning hide a key account's mailboxes. The review found the listing filter was not enforcement, so a use-time ownership rule (`za_foreign_key_account`, ruling R34) refuses every route into another account's key mailboxes, an impersonated token being foreign even on its own account (R35), and OAuth grants to an impersonated login are refused (R36).

**Task 8, search refusals (spec 8).** IMAP SEARCH, SORT and THREAD answer `NO [CANNOT]` for any content filter or sort on a key account; JMAP `Email/query`, `Email/queryChanges` and `SearchSnippet/get` answer `unsupportedFilter` or `unsupportedSort` naming the property; flag, UID, size and arrival-time searches keep working. The predicates are exhaustive matches over upstream's filter and sort enums (a variant added upstream stops the build there on purpose).

**Task 9, traces (spec 10, issues #20 and #21).** IMAP and POP3 trace a key account's session as `[redacted]` throughout (raw I/O, BAD errors, `Fetch` and `Store` details); SMTP redacts every read of a transaction delivering to or submitted by a key account (`za_key_rcpt`, `za_key_sender()`), with the DATA/BDAT forms matched as `is_auth_input` matches AUTH (ruling R41), the milter and spam-filter events likewise (`MilterClient::redact`, `SpamFilterInput::za_redact`, ruling R42), a key recipient after a BDAT chunk is refused with 503, and the vacation compile error reason names its position only (`compile error at line N, column M`) for every account. A mock milter is lifted into `tests/src/utils/milter.rs`. The LMTP test helper's read timeout is 10 s instead of 1.5 s (ruling R40).

**Task 10a, the key-mode suites and the leak extension (spec 11).** `key_accounts_mode()` moves to `tests/src/utils/za.rs`; `imap_tests` and `jmap_tests` provision their users as key accounts under `ZA_KEY_ACCOUNTS=1` and dispatch the flagged sub-modules to variants (the classification is below); `leak::scan` gains the mail rules (stub archives only, no `Threading` key, no term, `ReceivedAt` and `Size` only, no sample, no plaintext message blob, nothing held or queued after delivery) and `leak::test_mail` checks them with positive and negative controls and a canary sweep of IMAP, POP3 and SMTP events. Fifteen mutation runs; one equivalent mutant.

**Task 10b, docs, CI and this note.** The mail section of the manual checklist, CLAUDE.md (the keys invariant, the mail invariants bullet, the whole-session trace rule, the status paragraph, the merge hotspots, the suite paragraph), README-dev, the CI steps (key-mode IMAP and JMAP, then, by R45, their plain-mode steps and a 90-minute job timeout), and four comment fixes.

## Spec 13, the open items

How each item of spec section 13 resolved, as built. The plan's header resolved them against the code at `589e7f02`; where the build corrected the plan, "Plan corrections" says so.

1. **The stub's readers: one helper, `ZaMailView`.** A trait on `Server` (an inherent impl outside `common` is impossible), `za_metadata_view` returning `Cow<Archive<AlignedBytes>>` as `unseal_event_archive` does for events, called at IMAP FETCH, POP3 RETR and TOP, Email/get, SearchSnippet/get, EmailSubmission/set (`send_message` and `email_submission_set` gained the token parameter), `blob_download` and the index task; push, `copy_message` and `add_account_spam_sample` are gated or refused; unindex and account destruction read nothing sealed and are unchanged (Tasks 3, 4, 5).
2. **Restore re-parses: refused.** The key-account branch of `email_ingest` refuses `IngestSource::Restore` with a `MessageIngest(Error)` of code 550 and a `Security(Unauthorized)` event (`zero-access: restore of a key account's message`); `restore_item` turns it into a permanent task failure. Verbatim restore stays a packaging item (issue #25).
3. **The search document: built in the index task.** `build_email_document` returns a document holding `ReceivedAt` and `Size` (one extra `MessageData` read, no blob read) for a key account or a stub; ingest's batch stays upstream's (Task 5).
4. **The IMAP refusal reaches the client** as `<tag> NO [NOPERM] <text>` from `Session::authenticate`. The plan's "a `SecurityEvent` error closes the connection" was wrong; see "Plan corrections".
5. **SMTP DATA redaction: one read site, two flags** (`SessionData::za_key_rcpt`, set at RCPT by `Session::za_has_key_rcpt` through `Server::za_is_key_rcpt` (`crates/smtp/src/inbound/rcpt.rs`) and noted again at the end of DATA for recipients a milter, hook or script added (`crates/smtp/src/inbound/data.rs`); `za_key_sender()` for the authenticated sender), plus the pipelined forms of ruling R41 and the 503 for a key recipient after a BDAT chunk (Task 9).
6. **Push: confirmed.** The conversion at `push.rs` gives a key account's subscription the state-only payload; `build_email_push_object` also returns `Ok(None)` for a key account (Task 5).
7. **The `duplicate` Sieve test: hashed, nothing to gate.** `SeenIdHash::new` stores BLAKE3(account id, script hash, id) under `KV_SIEVE_ID`; the Message-ID is never stored.
8. **`Threading` clear on unindex: harmless, left as is.** The key is per document and never written for a stub; the leak test asserts no `Threading` key, and mutation 3b shows it would catch one.
9. **Upload coverage: one writer, two raw readers, confirmed.** `put_jmap_blob` is the only minter of a `Reserved` id (through `blob_upload` and `blob_upload_many`); raw readers were `blob_download`, the `Blob/upload` data sources, `FileNode/set` create and update, `FileNode/copy`, `Blob/copy`, `x:SpamTrainingSample/set` and `SieveScript/set` (Task 6; spec 5.7 and 9 list the `Blob/upload` data sources and `FileNode`, ruling R32 adds spam training and Sieve).
10. **Key-mode harness: `create_key_user_account` fits.** Both suites provision through it under `key_accounts_mode()`; the classification of Step 1 is below. The variants went beyond the plan's flagged list (`blob`, `file_node`, `calendars`, `principal_get` were added; `messagelimit` runs unchanged).
11. **The fence's plumbing: confirmed.** `deliver_message` maps a lost assertion to a temporary failure; `Store::is_pg_or_mysql()` feeds the 409; `Server::za_vault_delete` is the conditional delete (Task 4). The fence also covers iMIP's own batches (R25) and a reissue repairs a record left without a marker (R28).

## Rulings

The decisions block of the plan's header (D1-D6), then every ruling of the plan's ledger (R0-R45, in ledger order, amendments and clarifications kept with their ruling), each condensed to what was decided, why, and the cost if wrong; the final review's rulings R46-R57 are in its section. The ledger holds the full texts (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-20-zero-access-13-mail/progress.md`, untracked).

The plan's decisions:
- **D1.** The inner (sealed) metadata archive's `blob_hash` names the sealed blob, the same hash the stub carries, so the view's `blob_hash` is what readers fetch and the `mail-meta` associated data binds the stub to that blob. Cost if wrong: readers would fetch the wrong blob; none found.
- **D2.** Cross-account `Email/copy` and `Blob/copy` by or into a key account answer the method error `forbidden` (spec 6.3 says a set error): the call is invalid for that account pair, and the dispatcher gate already answers at the method level. Cost if wrong: one error shape.
- **D3.** Shared-mailbox listing exclusion lives in `build_access_token` (`Collection::Mailbox` and `Email` dropped from an `access_to` entry whose granting account is a key account), so every consumer sees no access. Task 7's review found it was a listing, not enforcement; R34 adds the use-time rule and D3 stays as the prompt listing. Cost if wrong: a stale listing.
- **D4.** `ImapEvent::Store`'s `Details` (keyword names) is redacted in a key session with `Fetch`'s although keyword names are visible by structure; mailbox names in `MailboxName` keys are routing structure and stay. Cost if wrong: dashboards lose keyword names for key sessions.
- **D5.** The spam-filter floor: `SpamFilterInput` gains `za_redact`; with it set the DNSBL `Hostname`, the LLM `Details` and the `Eval` result events are withheld; `Spam(Classify)`'s recipient list is transit metadata and stays. Cost if wrong: less diagnostic detail for key-account transactions.
- **D6.** The restore refusal and the "no served key" delivery failure are the ingest branch's own errors, so every caller of `email_ingest` sees one behaviour. Cost if wrong: none.

The ledger's rulings:
- **R0.** Reviewer tier is opus (the session ceiling) and the trailers are Opus 5.5; the plan header and constraints were amended, since the session model changed after approval. Cost: none. (Jay's direct request: the whole-branch review runs on fable, an explicit exception to the tier cap for that one dispatch; its fix wave and re-review stay opus.)
- **R1.** Task 1's tests copy `MessageMetadata` through an rkyv round trip (it derives no `Clone`); no derive is added to upstream types. Cost: a few test lines.
- **R2.** The fence assertion (`Principal`, document 0, `ZeroAccessVault`, `AssertValue::None`) is appended as the last ops of the ingest and copy batches, not after `with_account_id`: later ops would inherit its context. RocksDB, FoundationDB and SQLite apply a batch as one transaction (checked: `Batch::is_atomic()` has no caller; `Store::write` hands the whole batch to the backend), so the position does not weaken the fence. Cost: a reordered op, caught by the plain suites.
- **R3.** In the copy race, the parked `copy_message` fails with a server error (the lost assertion, surfacing as `serverUnavailable`) and stores nothing; it is the retry that answers `forbidden` (spec 6.1). Task 4 asserts the first, Task 7 the retry. Cost: one test expectation.
- **R4.** Task 5 defines `za_is_sealed_source` in full but converts only the Linked-Email branch of `blob_download`; the Reserved (upload) branch moves to Task 6 with the sealing writer, so uploads are never opened before they are sealed. Cost: none.
- **R5.** At provisioning, after `setup-token` writes the marker, the access tokens of every account named in the account's mailbox ACL grants are invalidated (the `refresh_acls` pattern, `za_mailbox_grantees`), so a grant made before provisioning stops routing. Cost: a stale listing until the grantee's token is rebuilt.
- **R6.** Task 2's key-residency tests clear the account's key-cache entries before each measurement and assert counts against a baseline, since HTTP logins legitimately cache keys. Cost: none.
- **R7.** A Bearer token comes from the real OAuth flow; the master-user case uses the recovery admin (`keymail1@example.com%admin`). Cost: one case.
- **R8.** Task 3's spam controls use user actions on the plain account (`STORE +FLAGS ($Junk)`, a MOVE to Junk), which write a sample when a classifier is configured; deliveries assert only that the key account gets none. The plan's plain control on delivery was vacuous, because ordinary delivery writes a sample only for card-is-ham and trusted replies. Cost: one control.
- **R9.** Task 4 step 4 asserts that a delivery to an account with a marker and a pending record gets a temporary failure (the key path's no-served-key failure, not the fence) and that the reissue answers 200. Cost: wording.
- **R10 (amended).** The stress test uses a one-sided order detector: the record writer reads, right after its commit, whether the account holds any Email archive (store level); a run whose read saw no message while the ingest returned `Ok` is a violation. Each run ends in one of three outcomes (the amendment added the third, because the writer writes the marker after the record): ingest `Ok` with a plaintext message, the lost assertion with no message, or the key path's no-served-key failure. It prints, without failing, when contention never produced the failure branch. Cost: a weaker stress signal; the hook tests are the deterministic evidence.
- **R11 (clarified).** `rf_2` asserts that the grantee's LIST and `Mailbox/get` no longer show the mailbox and that SELECT and APPEND by name answer `NO`; the keyless-stub refusal (`NO [NOPERM]`, the zero-access trace, never `DataCorruption`) is asserted on a session that had SELECTed the share before provisioning. The clarification: R5 may rebuild the token so a FETCH answers `NO` for lost access; in every case no `DataCorruption` and no plaintext, and when the read reaches the stub it is `NO [NOPERM]` with the trace. Cost: one case.
- **R12.** In Task 9 a lookup error while classifying an RCPT counts as a key recipient (fail closed). Cost: over-redaction of one transaction's DATA traces.
- **R13.** The per-task warning filter cannot print `clean` in the shipping configuration: it matches nine warnings that predate the branch (`common/src/auth/permissions.rs` 1, `common/src/auth/authentication.rs` 3, `services/src/task_manager/index.rs` 2, `jmap/src/registry/mapping/principal.rs` 1, `queued_message.rs` 2); a check passes when it prints nothing beyond them, compared by file and message. Cost: a misattributed pre-existing warning.
- **R14.** `stub_needs_private_key` keeps its `is_stub` check (a plaintext archive is `NotSealed`, as in `open_stub`); `blob_needs_private_key` keeps `Format`. Cost: one error variant.

## Spec notes

- Spec 5.2 says the stub check "runs only on key accounts' archives"; ruling R29 makes the view and the openers honour that literally: a `0x01`-headed archive is read as a stub only when the reader holds keys, and a keyless read of a key account's message is refused before the view runs.
- Spec 6.1 says a crash between the record write and the recheck leaves "a record without a marker, the state reissuance already repairs". It did not: a reissue answered 409 before any delete. Ruling R28 made the statement true. The same section's "once the record exists no ordinary writer can commit" holds for mail and iMIP only (R26's residual).
- Spec 9 gives `/jmap/upload` a 403; ruling R30 gives `/jmap/download` the same status for a keyless token, and the 403 body carries the client text.
- Spec 5.7 leaves Sieve scripts plaintext; ruling R32's amendment follows it (a key account's own uploaded script passes through `SieveScript/set`; only a message or a section of one is refused).
- Spec 10 says the structured-event floor applies to "an event emitted inside a key account's session". Task 9 extended the milter and spam-filter redaction to a transaction whose sender is a key account (`za_key_rcpt || za_key_sender()`), since a key account's own submission would otherwise log every header when a DATA-stage milter is enabled.
- `leak::test_mail` departs from the plan's letter in seven places, each forced by the code: the sub-address is `keymail12+mailrcpt-canary@example.com` (the plan's `mailrcpt-canary+keymail12@` is answered `550 5.1.2`); the custom header is `X-mailhdrname-canary` in the canary's own case (the search is case-sensitive); the `Threading` key lives in `SUBSPACE_PROPERTY` (`IndexPropertyClass::Hash`), not `SUBSPACE_INDEXES`, and the scanner checks both; the search document's value is the store's crate-private `TermIndex` (read by `crates/store/src/search/index.rs`, not `local.rs`), decoded by the scanner through a layout mirror (`ScannedDocument`) over `Archive::unarchive_untrusted`, with field keys checked for ids 10 and 12 as well; the submission goes to a second key account (`keymail13`), since a plain recipient would store and index the canary message in the clear; unlinked blobs are purged before the scan, because a delivered message's queue blob stays in the blob store until the purge; the queue and link rules count only after delivery completed (`Scan::undelivered`), as the plan's "after `wait_for_delivery`" says.
- `cargo test -p smtp has_data_command` needs `--features test_mode` to compile (an unrelated resolver mock); the plan's command omits it.
- Task 6 settled that a key account's `Reserved` id over a plaintext hash reads as not found.

- **Header item 4 (`SecurityEvent` errors).** The plan says a `SecurityEvent` error "is never written (`should_write_err`) and closes the connection".
